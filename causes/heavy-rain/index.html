What this cause can do to a property

Heavy Rain Water Damage can move beyond the first visible wet spot. Depending on location and duration, moisture may reach flooring, baseboards, drywall, cabinetry, insulation or structural cavities.

The key is to address the source when safe, remove affected water and assess how far moisture has traveled. If the source involves sewage or another contaminant, keep occupants away until a qualified provider advises on safe access.

Call details to gather

  • Exact room/location
  • How long it has been happening
  • Whether the source is active
  • Visible standing water
  • Any odor, discoloration or contamination

FAQ

Questions people ask before calling

What should I do first after heavy rain water damage?

If it is safe, stop the source and keep people away from unsafe or contaminated areas. Avoid electrical hazards and document the condition.

Can a small leak cause major damage?

Yes. A slow leak can wet concealed materials for a long time. The visible stain is not always the full extent of the moisture.

Should I clean contaminated water myself?

Do not handle sewage or heavily contaminated water without appropriate protection and procedures. Ask a qualified restoration provider for guidance.

Does this need water extraction?

If standing or trapped water is present, extraction or removal may be part of the response. The provider should determine the appropriate method.
